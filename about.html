My story

Different stages, same job: help people understand something and care about it.

Where it started

I trained as a puppetry actor at the Academy of Performing Arts in Bratislava. A puppet only comes alive when the audience believes in it. Getting there means watching people closely, timing everything, and cutting whatever doesn't land.

A new country, a new stage

I left Slovakia to see the world, work hard and build ambitious things with good people. In Denmark I studied Marketing Management at VIA University College, and learned Danish little by little.

From videos to the whole machine

At Trendhim I started by making videos and ended up running the organic content for five platforms. One Short about tying a tie has now passed 27 million views. It taught me that a practical answer to a real question beats a pretty video. See the Trendhim case

Design and marketing at one table

A Professional Bachelor in Digital Concept Development added UX/UI to the mix. Now I design how things work and how they're explained, and I'm looking for a team that cares about sustainability as much as I do.
